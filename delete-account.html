Delete your HappyHabit account

Last updated: 21 October 2026

HappyHabit is operated by Gansaa Labs. This page explains how to delete your HappyHabit account and what happens to your data.

In the app

  1. Open HappyHabit and go to Settings → Delete Account
  2. Enter your current password
  3. Type DELETE
  4. Tap "Permanently Delete My Account"

Your account is deleted straight away and cannot be recovered.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. To stop being charged, cancel it in Google Play (Play Store, profile icon, Payments & subscriptions, Subscriptions) before you delete your account.

Without the app

If you have uninstalled the app, or you can't log in, email support@happyhabit.org from the email address registered on the account, with the subject "Delete my account".

We will delete the account and confirm by email within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm that you own the account.

What is deleted

  • Your account and login
  • Children's profiles
  • Tasks and task history
  • Rewards and redemptions
  • Stars, bonus stars and setbacks
  • School events and calendar data
  • Stats and history

What may remain

  • Database backups, which are overwritten on a rolling basis within 30 days
  • Crash reports (email addresses removed), kept for up to 30 days as described in the Privacy Policy
  • Purchase records held by Google Play and RevenueCat under their own policies, which may be kept for legal and tax reasons
